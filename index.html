FAQ

?Does my audio ever get uploaded?

No. Recording, storage and transcription all happen on your computer. The only features that send anything over the network are ones you switch on yourself — GitLab sharing, calendar sync or a remote LLM — and they send text, never audio.

?Do I need to install anything else?

No. The transcription engine (whisper.cpp) ships inside the app. The first time you click Transcribe, it downloads its speech model once (about 550 MB) with a progress bar — after that everything works offline. Power users can plug in their own GPU engine (voxtype or a whisper.cpp build) and the app will use it automatically.

?Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the app and the speech model once. After that, recording, transcription and local summaries work fully offline. The calendar keeps each month on your device too, so it opens instantly and still shows your events offline.

?How do I update?

You don't have to come back here. When a new version is out, a notice appears in the sidebar — click Update & restart. The download is signature-checked before it installs, and updating is paused while you're recording or transcribing. You can also check by hand or turn off the launch check in Settings → App → Updates. On Linux the AppImage updates itself; .deb and .rpm installs get a Download button instead. Versions before 0.1.2 need one manual install to get the updater.

?Which calendars work?

Anything that speaks CalDAV — SOGo, Nextcloud, Zimbra, Kerio and others — plus Microsoft 365 (Graph) and on-prem Exchange (EWS). Your mail settings (IMAP/SMTP) don't carry calendars, so Settings offers shortcuts: Detect from server tries the usual addresses and lists your calendars, Find in Thunderbird reuses the calendars Thunderbird already knows (without passwords), and Use SOGo address fills it in from your host and username. A meeting can have several follow-up events, and each one is a draft until you approve it.

?Which speech model should I use?

Start with large-v3-turbo — it's the default and a great balance of speed and accuracy. For the best accuracy, parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3 sits at the top of the S-tier; on Linux it needs voxtype's ONNX variant. Settings ranks every model so the choice is obvious.

?Can it record Zoom, Meet or Teams calls?

Yes — choose Mic + Device and share the call's tab or window when asked. Your voice and the call are saved as separate tracks. Device-audio availability depends on your OS, and headphones are recommended so the mic doesn't pick up the speakers.

?What happens if transcription fails?

Nothing bad. The recording is the source of truth and is never modified by transcription. You'll see "Your original recording is still safe." and a Retry button.

?macOS says the app can't be opened — what now?

Early builds may not be notarized yet. Right-click the app in Applications and choose Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Local Transcribe.app".

?Is it really free?

Yes. Local Transcriber is open source under the MIT license. No tiers, no trial, no account.
